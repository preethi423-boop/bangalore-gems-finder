# City Compass

Create an app which reads the reviews, ratings of various places, parking slots near by that place, street food stalls, shoping places, special things that can be viewed, experience at that spot, across the Bangalore, near to Bangalore area and the app should show the search bar to search the place, it takes to the place similar to Google maps view, after selecting it gives the details of that places like parking place available, narrow roads, street food stalls special places andany more in the below of the window in carts or lines formate.
